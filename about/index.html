Placelgo is an algorithm that calculates the compatibility score between an individual and a place, based on their respective names.

We have built the algorithm by observing that most people seem to have more energy in some places.

You are welcome to test the validity of this algorithm for yourself and do your own tests among family members and friends.

Why should you use the Placealgo compatibility calculator?

  • To help you choose between various places to settle. You still need to listen to your heart, guts and brain.
  • So that you don't lose time, energy, or money moving to places that don't fit you.
  • To help you think about the various factors that come into play when choosing a place to settle.
  • It helps you explain why seemingly very different people can love the same place.
  • To minimize the chances of you having to move again, if you don't want to.
  • Most of the science behind Placealgo is applicable to relationships and career choices as well. In the same way you are dating and loving another inidvidual, you can date and love a place or a career.

The Placealgo algorithm was created by Henrik Hillerström, who was inspired by his fictive characters in The Grooters book series.
