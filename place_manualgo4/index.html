Place Compatibility Questionnaire

Welcome to Placealgo. Our aim is to help you consider all factors that come into play for choosing a place to live.

Think of a place that you consider moving to and answer the following questions.

Your Information

Place Information

Can be a continent, region, street name, or any other place-related name

Place-related Questionnaire

Answer the following questions about the place you're considering.

QuestionYesNo
1. Were you born or grew up there?
2. Do you speak the language there? Or are you eager to learn it?
3. Do you have the citizenship, or are you motivated to apply for a permanent residency visa?
4. Do you like the climate there? Or at least do you like most seasons there?
5. Do you like the location/topography? (sea, lake, river, forests, hills, mountain, countryside, ...)
6. Can you exert your favourite sports and hobbies there?
7. Can you exert your profession there?
8. Do you like the size of the place? (big city, medium town, small town, hamlet, ...)
9. Do you like the culture of the place? (architecture, food, fashion, traditions, people, ... incl. Indigenous culture)
10. Does the place have the amenities you need? (shops, schools, cafés, restaurants, cinemas, theatres, museums, hospital, stadium, swimming pool, ...)
11. Does the place have the infrastructure you need? (public transport, cycling paths, roads, public buildings, residential houses, ...)
12. Do you have friends and/or family living there or close by?
13. Do you have a professional network there?
14. Do you think you will feel safe there?
15. Do you think the place is or may be affordable for you to live in?
16. Are you OK with the country's politics?
